How to install

  1. Close CorelDRAW completely.
  2. Click Download installer, then open NestPro-Setup.exe.
  3. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.
  4. Click Next and wait until the installer finishes.
  5. Open CorelDRAW. The NestPro toolbar appears.
  6. Click any button. Enter your email, then the code we email you. You only do this once on each computer.

If the toolbar does not appear, right-click the toolbar area in CorelDRAW and tick NestPro.

To use NestPro on a different computer, click NestPro Account and choose Sign out on this one first.
